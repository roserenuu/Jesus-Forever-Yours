This Privacy Policy describes how Rose Renuu LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit jesusforeveryours.com, purchase our products, or use the Jesus Forever Yours mobile app. Sections 1–7 describe the website and book store. Section 8 describes the mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name and email address when you sign up for our email list or contact us
  • Billing and shipping information when you place an order (processed securely by Stripe)
  • Messages you send through our contact form

We also collect certain information automatically, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs through standard web analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Send you order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Send you love letters and faith content you've opted into
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Improve our website and offerings

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your information only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, including:

  • Stripe payment processing
  • Email service providers for sending newsletters and order emails
  • Shipping partners to fulfill hardcover orders

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may not use it for their own purposes.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your experience, analyze traffic, and understand how visitors interact with our site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. All payment data is handled exclusively by Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at support@jesusforeveryours.com or through our Contact page.

8. Jesus Forever Yours Mobile App

The app offers Bible reading, saved verses and notes, daily faith content, an AI companion called Faith, optional image and audio features, and optional purchases. You can use some features without an account. An account is needed for features that require sign-in.

App account and content

If you create an account, Supabase processes your email address, account identifier, authentication details, and profile information. Social sign-in providers may share information you authorize them to share. The app keeps a sign-in session on your device. Notes, saved verses, chat history, journal entries and photos, preferences, and Gems are stored on your device; profile information and content reports are also handled through Supabase. Removing the app may remove device-only content that has not been backed up elsewhere.

Faith, images, and audio

When you use Faith, your message, relevant conversation context, and the information needed to personalize or limit the feature are sent to our Railway-hosted backend. If you are signed in, requests can be associated with your account. The backend uses Anthropic to generate text responses. If you use Scan Verse, a photo you take or select is sent for image analysis. Generated carousel images are processed through OpenAI, and read-aloud audio through xAI. These providers process the content needed to deliver the feature. Please avoid putting sensitive personal information in messages or selected images.

Bible requests

When you open or search Bible passages, the app sends the passage reference or search text to Bolls (bolls.life) to return Scripture results. The service can receive technical request information such as your IP address.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles checkout in its app store. RevenueCat helps us display products, verify purchases, and manage Pro access and Gems. A signed-in app account identifier can be linked to purchase and subscription status. We do not receive your full payment-card number from the app stores.

Reports, diagnostics, and support

If you report an AI response or carousel, we receive the selected content, your reason and any note you add, your account identifier, and app version/platform so we can review it. Sentry processes crash and performance information and may receive your signed-in account identifier. The app sends onboarding and paywall usage events to our backend. If you contact support, we receive the information you provide in that message.

Your choices and data retention

You can choose whether to enable reminders or allow camera and selected-photo access. You can change permissions in your device settings. We keep account and profile information while your account is active, and device-only content remains on your device until you remove it, delete your account through the app, or uninstall the app. We keep reports, support requests, diagnostics, and transaction-related records as needed to operate the app, resolve issues, comply with applicable requirements, and meet the retention rules of the services described above. We do not run ads in the app, sell customer information, or use it for advertising outside the app.

Delete your app account

You can delete your account in the app or request help through our account-deletion page. We complete verified email deletion requests within 30 days. The in-app deletion flow removes the account from our Supabase authentication system and clears app-owned personal content from that device after the server confirms deletion. Some service-provider records and legally required transaction records may remain under their applicable retention rules. Deleting the app account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription; manage your subscription in the store where you purchased it.

Children and changes

The app is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us at support@jesusforeveryours.com so we can review and address it. We may update this policy as the app changes; the date above shows the latest revision.
