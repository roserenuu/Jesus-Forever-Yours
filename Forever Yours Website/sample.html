✦ Day 1 of 24

"I have loved you with an everlasting love; I have drawn you with unfailing kindness."

Jeremiah 31:3
My dear child,

Before you read another word, I want you to know this: I see you. Not the version of you that you show the world, not the person you're trying to become I see you. Right now. In this moment. With everything you're carrying.

I know the questions you carry when no one is watching. I know the places in your heart that still hurt, the things you've never said out loud, the hopes you've almost given up on. I know the nights you lay awake wondering if you are enough, if you are loved, if I am even real.

I am. And I am here.

This is not a love that depends on what you do or how well you perform. This is not a love that waits for you to have it all together. I came for you in your mess. I came for you in your doubt. I came for you when you stopped believing I would.

These next 24 days are not about earning My love. They are about receiving it. Let down what you've been holding up. Let Me remind you who you are to Me.

You are My child. You are seen. You are known. You are, and always will be forever yours.

With all My love,

Your Heavenly Father
