Bible verses for Women

These verses celebrate the strength, dignity, and worth of women in Scripture. They are a lovely gift for a mother, a friend, a daughter, or yourself.

Proverbs 31:25-26Psalm 46:5Proverbs 31:301 Peter 3:3-4Psalm 139:14Luke 1:45Song of Songs 4:7

She is clothed with strength and dignity, and she laughs without fear of the future. When she speaks, her words are wise, and she gives instructions with kindness.

Proverbs 31:25-26

This portrait of a woman of strength combines confidence and kindness. She faces the future without fear and speaks with wisdom. It celebrates inner strength expressed through gentle words.

ReflectWhich woman in your life reflects strength and kindness? Consider sharing this verse with her.

God dwells in that city; it cannot be destroyed. From the very break of day, God will protect it.

Psalm 46:5

In Psalm 46, God dwells within his city and protects it from the break of day. Many readers see in this verse a picture of God's steady presence in a life. It is a reminder that those God dwells with are secure.

ReflectHow might it encourage you to know God is present from the very break of day?

Charm is deceptive, and beauty does not last; but a woman who fears the Lord will be greatly praised.

Proverbs 31:30

This proverb contrasts charm and beauty, which fade, with reverence for God, which lasts. True praise belongs to character rooted in faith. It celebrates lasting beauty.

ReflectWhat character qualities would you most like to grow in, the kind of beauty that lasts?

Don’t be concerned about the outward beauty of fancy hairstyles, expensive jewelry, or beautiful clothes. You should clothe yourselves instead with the beauty that comes from within, the unfading beauty of a gentle and quiet spirit, which is so precious to God.

1 Peter 3:3-4

Peter points to the beauty that comes from within. A gentle and quiet spirit is precious to God. It is a reminder that God values the heart above appearance.

ReflectWhat does a gentle and quiet spirit look like in a busy, modern life?

Thank you for making me so wonderfully complex! Your workmanship is marvelous—how well I know it.

Psalm 139:14

David thanks God for making him wonderfully complex. It is a joyful recognition of God's careful design. It is a fitting verse to celebrate a birthday or a new life.

ReflectConsider writing this verse in a birthday card along with one specific quality you admire in the person.

You are blessed because you believed that the Lord would do what he said.”

Luke 1:45

Elizabeth spoke these words to Mary. She blessed Mary for believing God would keep his word. It celebrates the faith of a young woman who trusted God.

ReflectWhat promise of God are you trusting him to keep? Consider writing it down as an act of faith.

You are altogether beautiful, my darling, beautiful in every way.

Song of Songs 4:7

These words of affection are spoken within the Song of Songs. Many readers also hear in them an echo of how God sees his people. It is a tender affirmation of worth.

ReflectHow might it change your day to remember that you are deeply valued and loved?
A closer look

Going a little deeper.

Scripture honors women throughout its story, from Ruth's loyalty and Hannah's prayer to Mary's faith and Elizabeth's encouragement. These verses celebrate strength, dignity, wisdom, inner beauty, and faith. They make wonderful encouragement for a mother, daughter, sister, friend, or mentor, and they are popular for Mother's Day cards, women's events, and personal journaling. Proverbs 31 in particular paints a picture of a woman whose strength shows in many areas of life. Reading the full chapter offers a rich, balanced portrait of character rooted in reverence for God.

Each of these verses also makes a lovely journal prompt.

Ways to keep these verses close. Choose one verse to read each morning this week and notice how it shapes your day. Write a favorite verse on a card for your desk, mirror, or nightstand. Share one verse with a friend or family member who might enjoy it, along with a short personal note.

A prayer

Lord, thank you for making me with purpose. Clothe me with strength and dignity, and help me face the future with joy. Amen.
