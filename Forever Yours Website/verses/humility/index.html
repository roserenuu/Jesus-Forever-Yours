Bible verses About Humility

Humility is one of the most admired qualities in a person, and one of the hardest to practice on purpose. These verses show what humility looks like in daily life, from how we handle success to how we serve the people around us. They also show that humility is not thinking poorly of yourself. It is seeing yourself honestly before God and making room for others.

Proverbs 11:2Proverbs 22:4John 13:14-15James 4:101 Peter 5:5

Pride leads to disgrace, but with humility comes wisdom.

Proverbs 11:2

This proverb sets two paths side by side. Pride tends to end in embarrassment because it refuses to learn, while humility opens the door to wisdom. A humble person can say I was wrong, ask a question, or accept advice. Those small habits add up to a wiser life over time.

True humility and fear of the Lord lead to riches, honor, and long life.

Proverbs 22:4

Here humility is paired with the fear of the Lord, a deep respect for who God is. The proverb describes the kind of life that tends to grow from that posture, full of honor and good things. It is a general principle rather than a guarantee of wealth. The point is that humility is never wasted.

ReflectHow is your respect for God shaping the way you treat other people?

And since I, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you ought to wash each other’s feet. I have given you an example to follow. Do as I have done to you.

John 13:14-15

On the night before the cross, Jesus knelt and washed his disciples' feet, a task usually given to the lowest servant in the house. Then he told them to follow his example. Humility, in the life of Jesus, was not a feeling but an action. It looked like a towel, a basin, and a willingness to serve without being asked.

ReflectWhat is one quiet act of service you could do this week without being asked?

Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you up in honor.

James 4:10

James wrote to believers who were struggling with conflict and selfish ambition. His answer was to bring themselves low before God and let God do the lifting. That takes the pressure off trying to prove ourselves. Honor that comes from God is far more lasting than anything we could grab on our own.

ReflectIs there an area where you are trying to lift yourself up instead of trusting God with it?

In the same way, you who are younger must accept the authority of the elders. And all of you, dress yourselves in humility as you relate to one another, for “God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.”

1 Peter 5:5

Peter asks every believer to dress in humility, as though it were clothing put on fresh each day. He backs this up with a line from Proverbs that God opposes the proud and gives grace to the humble. Humility is how we relate to one another, young and old alike. It keeps relationships gentle and keeps our hearts open to God's grace.

ReflectWhat would it look like to put on humility each morning before you start your day?
A closer look

Going a little deeper.

People often search for verses about humility when preparing a lesson, writing a sermon, or working through a moment of pride or conflict. The passages above also pair well with the story of Jesus washing feet in John 13 and the description of his humility in Philippians 2. Reading one of them before an important meeting or a difficult conversation can change the tone of the whole day.

Humility is sometimes confused with low self worth, but the Bible presents it differently. A humble person knows they are deeply loved by God, which frees them from needing to win every argument or receive every compliment. That security makes it easier to celebrate others, admit mistakes, and serve gladly. Humility and confidence in God's love grow together.

Ways to keep these verses close. Choose one verse to read each morning this week and notice how it shapes your day. Write a favorite verse on a card for your desk, mirror, or nightstand. Share one verse with a friend or family member who might enjoy it, along with a short personal note.

A prayer

Lord, thank you for showing me what humility looks like through the life of Jesus. Clothe me in humility today. Help me listen more than I speak, admit when I am wrong, and serve without needing to be noticed. Guard my heart from pride, and teach me to find my worth in your love rather than in the approval of others. Lift me up in your time and in your way. Amen.
