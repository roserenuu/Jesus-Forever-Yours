A verse for today

Mercies new this morning.

If you came looking for a morning Bible verse, or a bible verse for today morning, begin here. Scripture does not ask the day to be easy. It says mercy is already waiting.

It is of the LORD’S mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

Lamentations 3:22-23 · KJV

For his anger endureth but a moment; in his favour is life: weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.

Psalm 30:5 · KJV

This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24 · KJV

For which cause we faint not; but though our outward man perish, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.

2 Corinthians 4:16 · KJV

Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the LORD: his going forth is prepared as the morning; and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth.

Hosea 6:3 · KJV

The just LORD is in the midst thereof; he will not do iniquity: every morning doth he bring his judgment to light, he faileth not; but the unjust knoweth no shame.

Zephaniah 3:5 · KJV

Lamentations was written in ruin, and still the writer says the mercies of the Lord are new every morning. That is a verse for today, not only for an easy day. The Jesus Forever Yours app carries the same promise into each sunrise: a letter, a verse, and a prayer.

Morning prayer scripture

Pray before the day begins.

A morning prayer scripture is simply a verse that already knows how to speak to God at daybreak. You do not need many words. These seven have been prayed for centuries.

My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O LORD; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.

Psalm 5:3 · KJV

Cause me to hear thy lovingkindness in the morning; for in thee do I trust: cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; for I lift up my soul unto thee.

Psalm 143:8 · KJV

O satisfy us early with thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.

Psalm 90:14 · KJV

But unto thee have I cried, O LORD; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee.

Psalm 88:13 · KJV

I prevented the dawning of the morning, and cried: I hoped in thy word.

Psalm 119:147 · KJV

O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is.

Psalm 63:1 · KJV

And in the morning, rising up a great while before day, he went out, and departed into a solitary place, and there prayed.

Mark 1:35 · KJV

David brings his requests in the morning and looks up. Jesus rises a great while before day and goes to a quiet place. If mornings are short, pray Psalm 143:8 as it stands. For more on talking with God, see our page on Bible verses about prayer.

Scripture

Sing in the morning.

Some mornings need a verse that lifts the eyes before the news does. Praise here is not a mood. It is a decision to name God’s love early.

But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble.

Psalm 59:16 · KJV

It is a good thing to give thanks unto the LORD, and to sing praises unto thy name, O most High: To shew forth thy lovingkindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness every night,

Psalm 92:1-2 · KJV

Awake up, my glory; awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early.

Psalm 57:8 · KJV

The Lord GOD hath given me the tongue of the learned, that I should know how to speak a word in season to him that is weary: he wakeneth morning by morning, he wakeneth mine ear to hear as the learned.

Isaiah 50:4 · KJV

Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O LORD, my strength, and my redeemer.

Psalm 19:14 · KJV

Isaiah’s servant is wakened morning by morning to hear. Psalm 19 asks that the first words of the day would be acceptable. You may also want verses for worship and praise and gratitude.

Scripture

Show me where to walk.

A bible verse for today morning is often a request for the next step. These verses ask God to direct the path, not merely to decorate the desk.

Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.

Proverbs 3:5-6 · KJV

But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

Matthew 6:33 · KJV

This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

Joshua 1:8 · KJV

Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: thy spirit is good; lead me into the land of uprightness.

Psalm 143:10 · KJV

Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

Isaiah 26:3 · KJV

Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.

Psalm 37:5 · KJV

Seek first the kingdom. Trust with all your heart. Commit your way. Direction in Scripture is received by staying close, not by guessing harder. For a longer look at wisdom, see Bible verses about wisdom.

Scripture

Strength for this day.

Some mornings already feel heavy. These verses ask God to be an arm every morning, a shepherd, a very present help.

O LORD, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble.

Isaiah 33:2 · KJV

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

Isaiah 41:10 · KJV

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

Philippians 4:6-7 · KJV

The LORD is my shepherd; I shall not want.

Psalm 23:1 · KJV

God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

Psalm 46:1 · KJV

You do not have to finish the day before you are held. If the morning is anxious, continue with Bible verses about strength, anxiety, or peace.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Morning in Scripture is not a productivity trick. It is the hour when mercies are named as new, when prayers rise before the day’s noise, and when God wakes the ear. A morning Bible verse does not have to be long. Read one slowly. Let it be the first voice you answer. Then step into the hours as someone who has already been met.

Keep a verse for the week on a card by the kettle. Or open the Jesus Forever Yours app: each day it meets you with a love letter from God, the verse of the day, and a prayer — the same gentle start these pages are built around.

A morning prayer

Father, this is the day you have made. Let me hear of your mercies this morning. Satisfy me with your love, show me where to walk, and be my strength today. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What is a good morning Bible verse for today?

Lamentations 3:22-23 is a beloved morning Bible verse: God’s mercies are new every morning. Psalm 118:24 and Psalm 143:8 are also a gentle start. The Jesus Forever Yours app brings a daily verse, a love letter, and a prayer each morning.

What is a morning prayer scripture?

Psalm 5:3, Psalm 143:8, and Psalm 90:14 are morning prayer scriptures — they ask God to hear us early, show us where to walk, and satisfy us with mercy. You can pray them as they are, or use the short morning prayer on this page.

How can I start my morning with the Bible?

Read one verse before the phone. Pray one sentence. Name one mercy. That is enough. The app was built around this simple rhythm: a letter from God, a verse, and a prayer to begin the day.

Which Bible translation is used here?

Every verse on this page is quoted from the King James Version, which is in the public domain. The Jesus Forever Yours app also includes the KJV and NLT, with more translations in Pro.
