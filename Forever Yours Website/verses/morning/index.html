A verse for today

Mercies new this morning.

If you came looking for a morning Bible verse, or a bible verse for today morning, begin here. Scripture does not ask the day to be easy. It says mercy is already waiting.

The faithful love of the Lord never ends! His mercies never cease. Great is his faithfulness; his mercies begin afresh each morning.

Lamentations 3:22 to 23 · NLT

For his anger lasts only a moment, but his favor lasts a lifetime! Weeping may last through the night, but joy comes with the morning.

Psalm 30:5 · NLT

This is the day the Lord has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24 · NLT

That is why we never give up. Though our bodies are dying, our spirits are being renewed every day.

2 Corinthians 4:16 · NLT

Oh, that we might know the Lord! Let us press on to know him. He will respond to us as surely as the arrival of dawn or the coming of rains in early spring."

Hosea 6:3 · NLT

But the Lord is still there in the city, and he does no wrong. Day by day he hands down justice, and he does not fail. But the wicked know no shame.

Zephaniah 3:5 · NLT

Lamentations was written in ruin, and still the writer says the mercies of the Lord are new every morning. That is a verse for today, not only for an easy day. The Jesus Forever Yours app carries the same promise into each sunrise: a letter, a verse, and a prayer.

Morning prayer scripture

Pray before the day begins.

A morning prayer scripture is simply a verse that already knows how to speak to God at daybreak. You do not need many words. These seven have been prayed for centuries.

Listen to my voice in the morning, Lord. Each morning I bring my requests to you and wait expectantly.

Psalm 5:3 · NLT

Let me hear of your unfailing love each morning, for I am trusting you. Show me where to walk, for I give myself to you.

Psalm 143:8 · NLT

Satisfy us each morning with your unfailing love, so we may sing for joy to the end of our lives.

Psalm 90:14 · NLT

O Lord, I cry out to you. I will keep on pleading day by day.

Psalm 88:13 · NLT

I rise early, before the sun is up; I cry out for help and put my hope in your words.

Psalm 119:147 · NLT

O God, you are my God; I earnestly search for you. My soul thirsts for you; my whole body longs for you in this parched and weary land where there is no water.

Psalm 63:1 · NLT

Before daybreak the next morning, Jesus got up and went out to an isolated place to pray.

Mark 1:35 · NLT

David brings his requests in the morning and looks up. Jesus rises a great while before day and goes to a quiet place. If mornings are short, pray Psalm 143:8 as it stands. For more on talking with God, see our page on Bible verses about prayer.

Scripture

Sing in the morning.

Some mornings need a verse that lifts the eyes before the news does. Praise here is not a mood. It is a decision to name God’s love early.

But as for me, I will sing about your power. Each morning I will sing with joy about your unfailing love. For you have been my refuge, a place of safety when I am in distress.

Psalm 59:16 · NLT

It is good to give thanks to the Lord, to sing praises to the Most High. It is good to proclaim your unfailing love in the morning, your faithfulness in the evening,

Psalm 92:1 to 2 · NLT

Wake up, my heart! Wake up, O lyre and harp! I will wake the dawn with my song.

Psalm 57:8 · NLT

The Sovereign Lord has given me his words of wisdom, so that I know how to comfort the weary. Morning by morning he wakens me and opens my understanding to his will.

Isaiah 50:4 · NLT

May the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be pleasing to you, O Lord, my rock and my redeemer.

Psalm 19:14 · NLT

Isaiah’s servant is wakened morning by morning to hear. Psalm 19 asks that the first words of the day would be acceptable. You may also want verses for worship and praise and gratitude.

Scripture

Show me where to walk.

A bible verse for today morning is often a request for the next step. These verses ask God to direct the path, not merely to decorate the desk.

Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take.

Proverbs 3:5 to 6 · NLT

Seek the Kingdom of God above all else, and live righteously, and he will give you everything you need.

Matthew 6:33 · NLT

Study this Book of Instruction continually. Meditate on it day and night so you will be sure to obey everything written in it. Only then will you prosper and succeed in all you do.

Joshua 1:8 · NLT

Teach me to do your will, for you are my God. May your gracious Spirit lead me forward on a firm footing.

Psalm 143:10 · NLT

You will keep in perfect peace all who trust in you, all whose thoughts are fixed on you!

Isaiah 26:3 · NLT

Commit everything you do to the Lord. Trust him, and he will help you.

Psalm 37:5 · NLT

Seek first the kingdom. Trust with all your heart. Commit your way. Direction in Scripture is received by staying close, not by guessing harder. For a longer look at wisdom, see Bible verses about wisdom.

Scripture

Strength for this day.

Some mornings already feel heavy. These verses ask God to be an arm every morning, a shepherd, a very present help.

But Lord, be merciful to us, for we have waited for you. Be our strong arm each day and our salvation in times of trouble.

Isaiah 33:2 · NLT

Don't be afraid, for I am with you. Don't be discouraged, for I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you. I will hold you up with my victorious right hand.

Isaiah 41:10 · NLT

Don't worry about anything; instead, pray about everything. Tell God what you need, and thank him for all he has done. Then you will experience God's peace, which exceeds anything we can understand. His peace will guard your hearts and minds as you live in Christ Jesus.

Philippians 4:6 to 7 · NLT

The Lord is my shepherd; I have all that I need.

Psalm 23:1 · NLT

God is our refuge and strength, always ready to help in times of trouble.

Psalm 46:1 · NLT

You do not have to finish the day before you are held. If the morning is anxious, continue with Bible verses about strength, anxiety, or peace.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Morning in Scripture is not a productivity trick. It is the hour when mercies are named as new, when prayers rise before the day’s noise, and when God wakes the ear. A morning Bible verse does not have to be long. Read one slowly. Let it be the first voice you answer. Then step into the hours as someone who has already been met.

Keep a verse for the week on a card by the kettle. Or open the Jesus Forever Yours app: each day it meets you with a love letter from God, the verse of the day, and a prayer: the same gentle start these pages are built around.

A morning prayer

Father, this is the day you have made. Let me hear of your mercies this morning. Satisfy me with your love, show me where to walk, and be my strength today. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What is a good morning Bible verse for today?

Lamentations 3:22 to 23 is a beloved morning Bible verse: God’s mercies are new every morning. Psalm 118:24 and Psalm 143:8 are also a gentle start. The Jesus Forever Yours app brings a daily verse, a love letter, and a prayer each morning.

What is a morning prayer scripture?

Psalm 5:3, Psalm 143:8, and Psalm 90:14 are morning prayer scriptures: they ask God to hear us early, show us where to walk, and satisfy us with mercy. You can pray them as they are, or use the short morning prayer on this page.

How can I start my morning with the Bible?

Read one verse before the phone. Pray one sentence. Name one mercy. That is enough. The app was built around this simple rhythm: a letter from God, a verse, and a prayer to begin the day.

Which Bible translation is used here?

Every verse on this page is quoted from the New Living Translation. The Jesus Forever Yours app also includes the NLT and KJV, with more translations in Pro.
