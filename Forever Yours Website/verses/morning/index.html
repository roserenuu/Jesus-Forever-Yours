Morning Bible Verses to Start Your Day

Many people like to start the day with Scripture before anything else. These short verses are a good way to begin a morning with God.

Psalm 5:3Lamentations 3:22-23Psalm 143:8Psalm 118:24Psalm 90:14Isaiah 33:2Psalm 59:16

Listen to my voice in the morning, Lord. Each morning I bring my requests to you and wait expectantly.

Psalm 5:3

David describes a morning habit of bringing requests to God. He waits expectantly, looking for God's response. It is a simple model for starting the day with prayer.

ReflectConsider bringing one request to God tomorrow morning and watching expectantly for his answer through the day.

The faithful love of the Lord never ends! His mercies never cease. Great is his faithfulness; his mercies begin afresh each morning.

Lamentations 3:22-23

These verses appear in a book written after the fall of Jerusalem, in the middle of deep lament. Right there, the writer remembers that God's mercies are new every morning. Hope in this passage is not denial of hardship but a fresh look at God's faithfulness each new day.

ReflectTomorrow morning, consider starting the day by reading this verse and naming one fresh mercy you notice before noon.

Let me hear of your unfailing love each morning, for I am trusting you. Show me where to walk, for I give myself to you.

Psalm 143:8

David asks to hear of God's unfailing love each morning. He also asks for direction for the day. It is a lovely prayer to begin a morning.

ReflectTry praying this verse as your first words of the morning tomorrow, asking for love and direction.

This is the day the Lord has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24

This verse is a joyful declaration that each day is a gift from God. Rejoicing is a choice made at the start of the day. It is a favorite verse for morning routines and celebrations alike.

ReflectWhat is one thing about today that you could rejoice in, even before the day fully begins?

Satisfy us each morning with your unfailing love, so we may sing for joy to the end of our lives.

Psalm 90:14

Moses, the author of Psalm 90, asks God to satisfy his people each morning. God's unfailing love is the source of lifelong joy. It is a morning prayer for contentment and gladness.

ReflectHow might being satisfied with God's love each morning shape the rest of your day?

But Lord, be merciful to us, for we have waited for you. Be our strong arm each day and our salvation in times of trouble.

Isaiah 33:2

Isaiah asks God to be his people's strong arm each day. The prayer recognizes that strength is needed daily, not just occasionally. It is a fitting prayer for the start of any day.

ReflectWhat part of your day would you most like God to be your strong arm in?

But as for me, I will sing about your power. Each morning I will sing with joy about your unfailing love. For you have been my refuge, a place of safety when I am in distress.

Psalm 59:16

David chooses to sing about God's power and love each morning. He remembers God as his refuge. It is a joyful way to start the day with praise.

ReflectConsider choosing one worship song to listen to tomorrow morning as a way of singing about God's love.
A closer look

Going a little deeper.

Many of the psalms were written with mornings in mind. David speaks of bringing requests to God at daybreak, and the writer of Lamentations celebrates mercies that are new each morning. Starting the day with a few minutes of Scripture, before the phone or the inbox, can set a steady tone for everything that follows. It does not need to be long. Reading one verse, praying one sentence, and naming one thing you are grateful for is a meaningful beginning. The Jesus Forever Yours app was designed around this kind of simple morning rhythm.

If mornings are busy, even reading a single verse while the coffee brews can make a difference.

Ways to keep these verses close. Choose one verse to read each morning this week and notice how it shapes your day. Write a favorite verse on a card for your desk, mirror, or nightstand. Share one verse with a friend or family member who might enjoy it, along with a short personal note.

A prayer
