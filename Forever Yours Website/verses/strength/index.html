Bible verses about strength

Strength in Scripture is rarely something we manufacture. It is something we receive. These thirty well known Bible verses about strength are for hard days, fearful nights, and the quiet weariness that does not always have a name.

Scripture

Strength in weakness.

Some of the most loved Bible verses about strength are not about being unbreakable. They are about a God who meets us where we run out.

Each time he said, "My grace is all you need. My power works best in weakness." So now I am glad to boast about my weaknesses, so that the power of Christ can work through me.

2 Corinthians 12:9 · NLT

That's why I take pleasure in my weaknesses, and in the insults, hardships, persecutions, and troubles that I suffer for Christ. For when I am weak, then I am strong.

2 Corinthians 12:10 · NLT

For I can do everything through Christ, who gives me strength.

Philippians 4:13 · NLT

He gives power to the weak and strength to the powerless.

Isaiah 40:29 · NLT

My health may fail, and my spirit may grow weak, but God remains the strength of my heart; he is mine forever.

Psalm 73:26 · NLT

Paul asked for the difficulty to be taken away, and the answer was grace instead. Strength here is not the absence of weakness. It is Christ present inside it.

Scripture

Fear and courage.

When the heart is afraid, Scripture does not scold. It names God as present, then invites us to stand.

Don't be afraid, for I am with you. Don't be discouraged, for I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you. I will hold you up with my victorious right hand.

Isaiah 41:10 · NLT

This is my command: be strong and courageous! Do not be afraid or discouraged. For the Lord your God is with you wherever you go."

Joshua 1:9 · NLT

So be strong and courageous! Do not be afraid and do not panic before them. For the Lord your God will personally go ahead of you. He will neither fail you nor abandon you."

Deuteronomy 31:6 · NLT

The Lord is my light and my salvation: so why should I be afraid? The Lord is my fortress, protecting me from danger, so why should I tremble?

Psalm 27:1 · NLT

For God has not given us a spirit of fear and timidity, but of power, love, and self-discipline.

2 Timothy 1:7 · NLT

So be strong and courageous, all you who put your hope in the Lord!

Psalm 31:24 · NLT

Courage in these verses is not a personality trait. It grows from a promise: God is with you. If fear is loud today, you may also want our pages on Bible verses about fear and anxiety.

Scripture

When you are weary.

Weariness is one of the most honest places to look for strength. These verses speak to tired bodies and tired souls.

But those who trust in the Lord will find new strength. They will soar high on wings like eagles. They will run and not grow weary. They will walk and not faint.

Isaiah 40:31 · NLT

Then Jesus said, "Come to me, all of you who are weary and carry heavy burdens, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you. Let me teach you, because I am humble and gentle at heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy to bear, and the burden I give you is light."

Matthew 11:28 to 30 · NLT

So let's not get tired of doing what is good. At just the right time we will reap a harvest of blessing if we don't give up.

Galatians 6:9 · NLT

God is our refuge and strength, always ready to help in times of trouble.

Psalm 46:1 · NLT

For I have given rest to the weary and joy to the sorrowing."

Jeremiah 31:25 · NLT

Isaiah's picture moves from soaring to running to walking. Strength is not only for dramatic days. It is also for the ordinary step. For more on rest, see verses for when you are tired.

Scripture

Strength in hard seasons.

Hard weeks need a place to stand. These verses call God a rock, a fortress, a stronghold, and the One who holds us through.

I love you, Lord; you are my strength. The Lord is my rock, my fortress, and my savior; my God is my rock, in whom I find protection. He is my shield, the power that saves me, and my place of safety.

Psalm 18:1 to 2 · NLT

The Lord is good, a strong refuge when trouble comes. He is close to those who trust in him.

Nahum 1:7 · NLT

The Sovereign Lord is my strength! He makes me as surefooted as a deer, able to tread upon the heights. (For the choir director: This prayer is to be accompanied by stringed instruments.)

Habakkuk 3:19 · NLT

The Lord is my strength and my song; he has given me victory. This is my God, and I will praise him: my father's God, and I will exalt him!

Exodus 15:2 · NLT

No, despite all these things, overwhelming victory is ours through Christ, who loved us.

Romans 8:37 · NLT

A stronghold is not a feeling. It is a place you can go. These verses give the heart somewhere to rest when the season does not change overnight. You may also find Bible verses about hope and encouraging Bible verses a gentle next step.

Scripture

The Lord is your strength.

Again and again, Scripture does not say “try harder.” It says the Lord himself is strength, song, shield, and joy.

And Nehemiah continued, "Go and celebrate with a feast of rich foods and sweet drinks, and share gifts of food with people who have nothing prepared. This is a sacred day before our Lord. Don't be dejected and sad, for the joy of the Lord is your strength!"

Nehemiah 8:10 · NLT

A final word: Be strong in the Lord and in his mighty power.

Ephesians 6:10 · NLT

The Lord is my strength and shield. I trust him with all my heart. He helps me, and my heart is filled with joy. I burst out in songs of thanksgiving.

Psalm 28:7 · NLT

The Lord is my strength and my song; he has given me victory.

Psalm 118:14 · NLT

See, God has come to save me. I will trust in him and not be afraid. The Lord God is my strength and my song; he has given me victory."

Isaiah 12:2 · NLT

The Lord gives his people strength. The Lord blesses them with peace.

Psalm 29:11 · NLT

Search for the Lord and for his strength; continually seek him.

1 Chronicles 16:11 · NLT

Then he said to me, "This is what the Lord says to Zerubbabel: It is not by force nor by strength, but by my Spirit, says the Lord of Heaven's Armies.

Zechariah 4:6 · NLT

The name of the Lord is a strong fortress; the godly run to him and are safe.

Proverbs 18:10 · NLT

Taken together, these lines are a kind of map: seek his face, run to his name, be strong in the Lord. Strength is received by coming close, not by manufacturing more willpower.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Across these passages, strength is a relationship before it is a feeling. Isaiah speaks of strength that is renewed. Paul speaks of power made perfect in weakness. The psalms call the Lord a rock and a shield. Taken together, they are good news for ordinary days as much as demanding ones. Before a hard conversation, a long shift, or a quiet morning that already feels heavy, it can help to read one verse slowly and ask God for the strength it describes.

A simple way to keep them close: choose one verse for the week. Write it on a card. Read it when you wake, and once more before you sleep. The Jesus Forever Yours app will also meet you with a love letter, a verse, and a prayer each morning.

A prayer

Father, thank you that your strength is not far off. Meet me in weakness, quiet my fear, and renew me where I am tired. Be the strength of my heart today. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What are the best Bible verses about strength?

Some of the most loved are Isaiah 40:31, Philippians 4:13, Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 46:1, and 2 Corinthians 12:9. This page gathers thirty well known passages, grouped for weakness, fear, weariness, and hard seasons.

What Bible verse says I can do all things through Christ?

Philippians 4:13 in the New Living Translation reads, “For I can do everything through Christ, who gives me strength.” In context, Paul is speaking about contentment in plenty and in need, not only about big achievements.

Where should I start when I feel weak?

Begin with 2 Corinthians 12:9 and Isaiah 40:29. They speak to faintness rather than to performance. Then pray the short prayer on this page, or open the Jesus Forever Yours app for a daily letter, verse, and prayer.

Which Bible translation is used here?

Every verse on this page is quoted from the New Living Translation. The Jesus Forever Yours app also includes the NLT and KJV, with more translations in Pro.
