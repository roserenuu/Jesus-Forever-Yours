Bible verses about strength

Strength in Scripture is rarely something we manufacture. It is something we receive. These thirty well-known Bible verses about strength are for hard days, fearful nights, and the quiet weariness that does not always have a name.

Scripture

Strength in weakness.

Some of the most loved Bible verses about strength are not about being unbreakable. They are about a God who meets us where we run out.

And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.

2 Corinthians 12:9 · KJV

Therefore I take pleasure in infirmities, in reproaches, in necessities, in persecutions, in distresses for Christ's sake: for when I am weak, then am I strong.

2 Corinthians 12:10 · KJV

I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.

Philippians 4:13 · KJV

He giveth power to the faint; and to them that have no might he increaseth strength.

Isaiah 40:29 · KJV

My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.

Psalm 73:26 · KJV

Paul asked for the difficulty to be taken away, and the answer was grace instead. Strength here is not the absence of weakness. It is Christ present inside it.

Scripture

Fear and courage.

When the heart is afraid, Scripture does not scold. It names God as present, then invites us to stand.

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

Isaiah 41:10 · KJV

Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

Joshua 1:9 · KJV

Be strong and of a good courage, fear not, nor be afraid of them: for the LORD thy God, he it is that doth go with thee; he will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.

Deuteronomy 31:6 · KJV

The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?

Psalm 27:1 · KJV

For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

2 Timothy 1:7 · KJV

Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the LORD.

Psalm 31:24 · KJV

Courage in these verses is not a personality trait. It grows from a promise: God is with you. If fear is loud today, you may also want our pages on Bible verses about fear and anxiety.

Scripture

When you are weary.

Weariness is one of the most honest places to look for strength. These verses speak to tired bodies and tired souls.

But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.

Isaiah 40:31 · KJV

Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

Matthew 11:28-30 · KJV

And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

Galatians 6:9 · KJV

God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

Psalm 46:1 · KJV

For I have satiated the weary soul, and I have replenished every sorrowful soul.

Jeremiah 31:25 · KJV

Isaiah's picture moves from soaring to running to walking. Strength is not only for dramatic days. It is also for the ordinary step. For more on rest, see verses for when you are tired.

Scripture

Strength in hard seasons.

Hard weeks need a place to stand. These verses call God a rock, a fortress, a stronghold, and the One who holds us through.

I will love thee, O LORD, my strength. The LORD is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower.

Psalm 18:1-2 · KJV

The LORD is good, a strong hold in the day of trouble; and he knoweth them that trust in him.

Nahum 1:7 · KJV

The LORD God is my strength, and he will make my feet like hinds' feet, and he will make me to walk upon mine high places.

Habakkuk 3:19 · KJV

The LORD is my strength and song, and he is become my salvation: he is my God, and I will prepare him an habitation; my father's God, and I will exalt him.

Exodus 15:2 · KJV

Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.

Romans 8:37 · KJV

A stronghold is not a feeling. It is a place you can go. These verses give the heart somewhere to rest when the season does not change overnight. You may also find Bible verses about hope and encouraging Bible verses a gentle next step.

Scripture

The Lord is your strength.

Again and again, Scripture does not say “try harder.” It says the Lord himself is strength, song, shield, and joy.

Then he said unto them, Go your way, eat the fat, and drink the sweet, and send portions unto them for whom nothing is prepared: for this day is holy unto our Lord: neither be ye sorry; for the joy of the LORD is your strength.

Nehemiah 8:10 · KJV

Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might.

Ephesians 6:10 · KJV

The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him.

Psalm 28:7 · KJV

The LORD is my strength and song, and is become my salvation.

Psalm 118:14 · KJV

Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust, and not be afraid: for the LORD JEHOVAH is my strength and my song; he also is become my salvation.

Isaiah 12:2 · KJV

The LORD will give strength unto his people; the LORD will bless his people with peace.

Psalm 29:11 · KJV

Seek the LORD and his strength, seek his face continually.

1 Chronicles 16:11 · KJV

Then he answered and spake unto me, saying, This is the word of the LORD unto Zerubbabel, saying, Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts.

Zechariah 4:6 · KJV

The name of the LORD is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.

Proverbs 18:10 · KJV

Taken together, these lines are a kind of map: seek his face, run to his name, be strong in the Lord. Strength is received by coming close, not by manufacturing more willpower.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Across these passages, strength is a relationship before it is a feeling. Isaiah speaks of strength that is renewed. Paul speaks of power made perfect in weakness. The psalms call the Lord a rock and a shield. Taken together, they are good news for ordinary days as much as demanding ones. Before a hard conversation, a long shift, or a quiet morning that already feels heavy, it can help to read one verse slowly and ask God for the strength it describes.

A simple way to keep them close: choose one verse for the week. Write it on a card. Read it when you wake, and once more before you sleep. The Jesus Forever Yours app will also meet you with a love letter, a verse, and a prayer each morning.

A prayer

Father, thank you that your strength is not far off. Meet me in weakness, quiet my fear, and renew me where I am tired. Be the strength of my heart today. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What are the best Bible verses about strength?

Some of the most loved are Isaiah 40:31, Philippians 4:13, Isaiah 41:10, Psalm 46:1, and 2 Corinthians 12:9. This page gathers thirty well-known passages, grouped for weakness, fear, weariness, and hard seasons.

What Bible verse says I can do all things through Christ?

Philippians 4:13 in the King James Version reads, “I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.” In context, Paul is speaking about contentment in plenty and in need, not only about big achievements.

Where should I start when I feel weak?

Begin with 2 Corinthians 12:9 and Isaiah 40:29. They speak to faintness rather than to performance. Then pray the short prayer on this page, or open the Jesus Forever Yours app for a daily letter, verse, and prayer.

Which Bible translation is used here?

Every verse on this page is quoted from the King James Version, which is in the public domain. The Jesus Forever Yours app also includes the KJV and NLT, with more translations in Pro.
