Bible verses About Patience and Waiting

Waiting is part of nearly every story in Scripture. These verses offer encouragement for seasons of waiting on God.

Psalm 27:14Lamentations 3:25-26Romans 12:12Galatians 6:9Psalm 37:7James 5:7-8

Wait patiently for the Lord. Be brave and courageous. Yes, wait patiently for the Lord.

Psalm 27:14

David repeats the call to wait patiently for the Lord. Courage and waiting go together. The repetition emphasizes how important patient trust is.

ReflectWhat are you waiting for right now, and how might courage and patience work together in that waiting?

The Lord is good to those who depend on him, to those who search for him. So it is good to wait quietly for salvation from the Lord.

Lamentations 3:25-26

The writer describes God as good to those who seek him. Waiting quietly is presented as good. It is a reminder that God is worth waiting for.

ReflectHow might quiet waiting become a form of trust rather than frustration in your current season?

Rejoice in our confident hope. Be patient in trouble, and keep on praying.

Romans 12:12

Paul pairs three simple habits, rejoicing, patience, and prayer. Each supports the others. It is a short but complete guide for every season.

ReflectWhich of the three habits, rejoicing, patience, or prayer, could you strengthen this week?

So let’s not get tired of doing what is good. At just the right time we will reap a harvest of blessing if we don’t give up.

Galatians 6:9

Paul encourages readers to keep doing good even when results take time. He uses the image of a harvest that comes at just the right time. It is a reminder that faithful effort is never wasted.

ReflectWhat good work are you continuing, even if results have not appeared yet? Let this verse encourage you to keep going.

Be still in the presence of the Lord, and wait patiently for him to act. Don’t worry about evil people who prosper or fret about their wicked schemes.

Psalm 37:7

David encourages being still in God's presence. Patient waiting replaces worry about others. It is an invitation to rest in God's timing.

ReflectConsider spending five minutes in stillness before God today, simply resting in his presence.

Dear brothers and sisters, be patient as you wait for the Lord’s return. Consider the farmers who patiently wait for the rains in the fall and in the spring. They eagerly look for the valuable harvest to ripen. You, too, must be patient. Take courage, for the coming of the Lord is near.

James 5:7-8

James uses the image of a farmer waiting for rain and harvest. Patience is active, expectant waiting. The coming of the Lord gives courage. The farmer prepares the soil and trusts the seasons, and readers are invited to trust God the same way.

ReflectWhat preparation, like the farmer readying the field, could you do while you wait for God's timing? Consider which part of your life is in a season of planting and which is in a season of waiting for harvest.
A closer look

Going a little deeper.

Waiting is part of nearly every story in the Bible. Abraham waited for a promised son, Israel waited in the wilderness, and the early church waited for the Spirit. These verses show that waiting on God is active and hopeful rather than idle. It involves prayer, courage, stillness, and trust in his timing. James compares it to a farmer who prepares the field and watches for rain. If you are in a season of waiting, choosing one verse to return to each day can help anchor your heart while you wait.

Patience is listed in Galatians 5 as part of the fruit of the Spirit, which means it is something God grows in us over time rather than something we must produce on our own. It often develops quietly, in traffic, in lines, in long conversations, and in seasons of waiting. These verses can be a gentle companion for each of those moments, reminding us that God is good, present, and at work.

Ways to keep these verses close. Choose one verse to read each morning this week and notice how it shapes your day. Write a favorite verse on a card for your desk, mirror, or nightstand. Share one verse with a friend or family member who might enjoy it, along with a short personal note.

A prayer

Lord, help me wait on you with patience and hope. Strengthen my heart while I wait, and remind me that your timing is good. Amen.
