Bible verses for Men

Scripture speaks to men about courage, integrity, friendship, and faithful love. These verses are for fathers, sons, husbands, brothers, and friends at every stage of life, and they work well for a men's group, a father's card, or a personal morning reading.

1 Corinthians 16:13-14Psalm 37:23-24Proverbs 20:71 Timothy 6:11Galatians 6:2

Be on guard. Stand firm in the faith. Be courageous. Be strong. And do everything with love.

1 Corinthians 16:13-14

Paul closes his letter to the church in Corinth with five short commands, almost like a coach's final words before a game. Be alert, stand firm, be courageous, be strong. Then he adds the line that holds it all together, do everything with love. Strength in Scripture is never harsh or self centered. It is courage used in the service of love.

ReflectWhere could you show courage today in a way that is also full of love?

The Lord directs the steps of the godly. He delights in every detail of their lives. Though they stumble, they will never fall, for the Lord holds them by the hand.

Psalm 37:23-24

David wrote Psalm 37 later in life, looking back on years of watching God's faithfulness. He says the Lord directs the steps of the godly and delights in the details of their lives. The verses are honest that people stumble. But a stumble is not a final fall, because the Lord holds them by the hand. Faithfulness is a walk with God, not a perfect record.

ReflectWhere have you seen God direct your steps, even through a stumble, in the past year?

The godly walk with integrity; blessed are their children who follow them.

Proverbs 20:7

This proverb links personal integrity with the blessing of the next generation. Children, and anyone who looks up to us, learn by watching how we live, whether we keep our word, treat people fairly, and own our mistakes. Integrity means being the same person in public and in private. Over a lifetime, it becomes a legacy others can follow.

ReflectWhat is one habit of integrity you would like the people who look up to you to see?

But you, Timothy, are a man of God; so run from all these evil things. Pursue righteousness and a godly life, along with faith, love, perseverance, and gentleness.

1 Timothy 6:11

Paul calls Timothy a man of God and gives him a clear direction. In the verses just before, Paul warned about the love of money and the trouble it brings. Instead of chasing those things, Timothy is to pursue six qualities, righteousness, godliness, faith, love, perseverance, and gentleness. Gentleness sits right beside perseverance on the list, a balanced picture of godly character.

ReflectWhich of the six qualities in this verse would you like to pursue more intentionally this month?

Share each other’s burdens, and in this way obey the law of Christ.

Galatians 6:2

Paul asks believers to carry one another's burdens, which fulfills the law of Christ, the command to love one another. Many men find it easier to help than to ask for help. This verse makes both sides normal. Sharing burdens might mean helping a neighbor move, checking in on a friend, or letting a trusted friend help carry something heavy.

ReflectWhich friend could you check in on this week, and is there a burden you could let someone help you carry?
A closer look

Going a little deeper.

The Bible offers many examples of men who walked with God, and very few of them were flawless. Abraham trusted God with an unknown future, Joseph forgave the brothers who sold him, David wrote psalms of honest prayer, Nehemiah led a city through a huge rebuilding project, and Peter grew from impulsive fisherman to steady leader. Their stories show that godly character grows over time through faith, repentance, and practice. Jesus himself joined strength with compassion, welcoming children, touching the sick, and standing firm under pressure.

Friendship and accountability are a recurring theme as well. Proverbs speaks of iron sharpening iron, and Ecclesiastes notes that two are better than one. Many men find it helpful to meet regularly with a friend or small group to read Scripture, pray, and talk honestly about life. Even a short weekly conversation over coffee can be a practical way to live out these verses together.

Ways to keep these verses close. Choose one verse to read each morning this week and notice how it shapes your day. Write a favorite verse on a card for your desk, mirror, or nightstand. Share one verse with a friend or family member who might enjoy it, along with a short personal note.

A prayer

Lord, thank you for walking with me and holding my hand. Make me courageous and strong, and help me do everything with love. Shape me into a man of integrity, gentleness, and faith. Give me good friends to share life with, and help me carry others' burdens well. Amen.
