Birthday Bible verses and blessings

Scripture

Wonderfully made.

A birthday is a good day to remember that a life is not an accident. These verses thank God for forming, knowing, and giving.

For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother’s womb. I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well.

Psalm 139:13-14 · KJV

Thine eyes did see my substance, yet being unperfect; and in thy book all my members were written, which in continuance were fashioned, when as yet there was none of them.

Psalm 139:16 · KJV

Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations.

Jeremiah 1:5 · KJV

For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.

Ephesians 2:10 · KJV

Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and cometh down from the Father of lights, with whom is no variableness, neither shadow of turning.

James 1:17 · KJV

By thee have I been holden up from the womb: thou art he that took me out of my mother’s bowels: my praise shall be continually of thee.

Psalm 71:6 · KJV

Psalm 139 is at home in a child’s card and in an adult’s. Add one specific quality you love, and the verse becomes a blessing, not only a quotation. You may also like who God says you are.

Scripture

Blessing for the years.

These are Bible verses for birthdays blessing in the older sense: words spoken over someone, asking God to keep, lengthen, and be near.

The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace.

Numbers 6:24-26 · KJV

Grant thee according to thine own heart, and fulfil all thy counsel.

Psalm 20:4 · KJV

With long life will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation.

Psalm 91:16 · KJV

For by me thy days shall be multiplied, and the years of thy life shall be increased.

Proverbs 9:11 · KJV

Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth.

3 John 1:2 · KJV

Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore.

Psalm 16:11 · KJV

And even to your old age I am he; and even to hoar hairs will I carry you: I have made, and I will bear; even I will carry, and will deliver you.

Isaiah 46:4 · KJV

The hoary head is a crown of glory, if it be found in the way of righteousness.

Proverbs 16:31 · KJV

Numbers 6 has been spoken over people for more than three thousand years. Isaiah 46 and Proverbs 16 sit well in a card for someone growing older. See also verses for growing older.

Scripture

Hope for the year ahead.

A birthday looks forward as much as it looks back. These verses bless the next twelve months with peace, wisdom, and a God who finishes what he starts.

For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.

Jeremiah 29:11 · KJV

This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24 · KJV

So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.

Psalm 90:12 · KJV

Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ:

Philippians 1:6 · KJV

Delight thyself also in the LORD; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart.

Psalm 37:4 · KJV

Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.

Proverbs 3:5-6 · KJV

O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever.

1 Chronicles 16:34 · KJV

The LORD thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.

Zephaniah 3:17 · KJV

Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits: Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases; Who redeemeth thy life from destruction; who crowneth thee with lovingkindness and tender mercies; Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things; so that thy youth is renewed like the eagle’s.

Psalm 103:2-5 · KJV

Jeremiah 29:11 was first sent to people in a long wait. It is still a kind word for a new year of life. For more on looking ahead with God, see God’s plan and purpose and hope.

For cards

Birthday blessings.

If you searched for bible verses for birthdays blessing, these short lines are ready to write. They grow from the scriptures above. Copy one, then add a sentence in your own voice.

The LORD bless thee, and keep thee this year. May he make his face shine upon you, and give you peace.

A blessing from Numbers 6:24-26

You are fearfully and wonderfully made. Today I thank God for the care with which he wrote your life.

A blessing from Psalm 139:14

May the year ahead hold his thoughts of peace, and a future with hope.

A blessing from Jeremiah 29:11

May he grant your heart’s desire and fulfil all your counsel.

A blessing from Psalm 20:4

Every good gift is from above. Today we thank him for the gift of you.

A blessing from James 1:17

May he satisfy you with long life, and shew you his salvation.

A blessing from Psalm 91:16

A card does not need many sentences. One blessing, one memory, and a verse is enough. If you are celebrating a wedding year instead, see wedding Bible verses and verses for marriage.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Scripture never treats a birthday as a small thing. It numbers days, blesses years, and calls each person fearfully and wonderfully made. The kindest cards do two jobs at once: they thank God for the life, and they ask him to keep it. Whether you write to a child, a parent, or a friend, let the verse carry the weight and let your own sentence make it personal.

Keep a favorite blessing on a note in your phone for the next invitation. Or open the Jesus Forever Yours app and send someone the day’s verse with a line of your own.

A prayer

Lord, thank you for this life and for another year of it. Bless them and keep them. Make your face shine upon them, and give them peace. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What are the best Bible verses for a birthday card?

Numbers 6:24-26, Psalm 139:14, Jeremiah 29:11, and Psalm 20:4 are among the most loved. This page gathers twenty-three passages, plus short birthday blessings you can copy into a card.

What Bible verse is a blessing for a birthday?

The priestly blessing in Numbers 6:24-26 is the classic scripture blessing: “The LORD bless thee, and keep thee.” Psalm 91:16 and 3 John 1:2 also read naturally as a prayer over someone’s year.

Which verse is good for a child’s birthday?

Psalm 139:13-14 thanks God for forming us with care. Jeremiah 1:5 and Ephesians 2:10 also remind a child that their life is known and made on purpose. Add one sentence about what you love in them.

Which Bible translation is used here?

Every verse on this page is quoted from the King James Version, which is in the public domain. The Jesus Forever Yours app also includes the KJV and NLT, with more translations in Pro.
