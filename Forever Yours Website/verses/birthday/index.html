Scripture

Wonderfully made.

A birthday is a good day to remember that a life is not an accident. These verses thank God for forming, knowing, and giving.

May the Lord bless you and protect you this year. May he smile on you and be gracious to you, and give you peace.

A blessing from Numbers 6:24 to 26

You are wonderfully complex, and his workmanship is marvelous. Today I thank God for the care with which he wrote your life.

A blessing from Psalm 139:14

May the year ahead hold his plans for good, and a future filled with hope.

A blessing from Jeremiah 29:11

May he grant your heart's desires and make all your plans succeed.

A blessing from Psalm 20:4

Whatever is good and perfect is a gift coming down to us from God. Today we thank him for the gift of you.

A blessing from James 1:17

May he reward you with a long life and give you his salvation.

A blessing from Psalm 91:16

A card does not need many sentences. One blessing, one memory, and a verse is enough. If you are celebrating a wedding year instead, see wedding Bible verses and verses for marriage.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Scripture never treats a birthday as a small thing. It numbers days, blesses years, and calls each person fearfully and wonderfully made. The kindest cards do two jobs at once: they thank God for the life, and they ask him to keep it. Whether you write to a child, a parent, or a friend, let the verse carry the weight and let your own sentence make it personal.

Keep a favorite blessing on a note in your phone for the next invitation. Or open the Jesus Forever Yours app and send someone the day’s verse with a line of your own.

A prayer

Lord, thank you for this life and for another year of it. Bless them and keep them. Make your face shine upon them, and give them peace. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What are the best Bible verses for a birthday card?

Numbers 6:24 to 26, Psalm 139:14, Jeremiah 29:11, and Psalm 20:4 are among the most loved. This page gathers twenty three passages, plus short birthday blessings you can copy into a card.

What Bible verse is a blessing for a birthday?

The priestly blessing in Numbers 6:24 to 26 is the classic scripture blessing: “May the Lord bless you and protect you.” Psalm 91:16 and 3 John 1:2 also read naturally as a prayer over someone’s year.

Which verse is good for a child’s birthday?

Psalm 139:13 to 14 thanks God for forming us with care. Jeremiah 1:5 and Ephesians 2:10 also remind a child that their life is known and made on purpose. Add one sentence about what you love in them.

Which Bible translation is used here?

Every verse on this page is quoted from the New Living Translation. The Jesus Forever Yours app also includes the NLT and KJV, with more translations in Pro.
