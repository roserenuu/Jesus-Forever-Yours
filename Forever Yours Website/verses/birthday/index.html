Birthday Bible verses and blessings

A birthday is a good day to give thanks for a life. These twenty three New Living Translation verses, and the short birthday blessings beneath them, are for cards, messages, and prayers: scripture you can write in a single sitting.

Scripture

Wonderfully made.

A birthday is a good day to remember that a life is not an accident. These verses thank God for forming, knowing, and giving.

You made all the delicate, inner parts of my body and knit me together in my mother’s womb. Thank you for making me so wonderfully complex! Your workmanship is marvelous: how well I know it.

Psalm 139:13 to 14 · NLT

You saw me before I was born. Every day of my life was recorded in your book. Every moment was laid out before a single day had passed.

Psalm 139:16 · NLT

“I knew you before I formed you in your mother’s womb. Before you were born I set you apart and appointed you as my prophet to the nations.”

Jeremiah 1:5 · NLT

For we are God’s masterpiece. He has created us anew in Christ Jesus, so we can do the good things he planned for us long ago.

Ephesians 2:10 · NLT

Whatever is good and perfect is a gift coming down to us from God our Father, who created all the lights in the heavens. He never changes or casts a shifting shadow.

James 1:17 · NLT

Yes, you have been with me from birth; from my mother’s womb you have cared for me. No wonder I am always praising you!

Psalm 71:6 · NLT

Psalm 139 is at home in a child’s card and in an adult’s. Add one specific quality you love, and the verse becomes a blessing, not only a quotation. You may also like who God says you are.

Scripture

Blessing for the years.

These are Bible verses for birthdays blessing in the older sense: words spoken over someone, asking God to keep, lengthen, and be near.

‘May the Lord bless you and protect you. May the Lord smile on you and be gracious to you. May the Lord show you his favor and give you his peace.’

Numbers 6:24 to 26 · NLT

May he grant your heart’s desires and make all your plans succeed.

Psalm 20:4 · NLT

I will reward them with a long life and give them my salvation.”

Psalm 91:16 · NLT

Wisdom will multiply your days and add years to your life.

Proverbs 9:11 · NLT

Dear friend, I hope all is well with you and that you are as healthy in body as you are strong in spirit.

3 John 1:2 · NLT

You will show me the way of life, granting me the joy of your presence and the pleasures of living with you forever.

Psalm 16:11 · NLT

I will be your God throughout your lifetime: until your hair is white with age. I made you, and I will care for you. I will carry you along and save you.

Isaiah 46:4 · NLT

Gray hair is a crown of glory; it is gained by living a godly life.

Proverbs 16:31 · NLT

Numbers 6 has been spoken over people for more than three thousand years. Isaiah 46 and Proverbs 16 sit well in a card for someone growing older. See also verses for growing older.

Scripture

Hope for the year ahead.

A birthday looks forward as much as it looks back. These verses bless the next twelve months with peace, wisdom, and a God who finishes what he starts.

For I know the plans I have for you,” says the Lord. “They are plans for good and not for disaster, to give you a future and a hope.

Jeremiah 29:11 · NLT

This is the day the Lord has made. We will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24 · NLT

Teach us to realize the brevity of life, so that we may grow in wisdom.

Psalm 90:12 · NLT

And I am certain that God, who began the good work within you, will continue his work until it is finally finished on the day when Christ Jesus returns.

Philippians 1:6 · NLT

Take delight in the Lord, and he will give you your heart’s desires.

Psalm 37:4 · NLT

Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take.

Proverbs 3:5 to 6 · NLT

Give thanks to the Lord, for he is good! His faithful love endures forever.

1 Chronicles 16:34 · NLT

For the Lord your God is living among you. He is a mighty savior. He will take delight in you with gladness. With his love, he will calm all your fears. He will rejoice over you with joyful songs.”

Zephaniah 3:17 · NLT

Let all that I am praise the Lord; may I never forget the good things he does for me. He forgives all my sins and heals all my diseases. He redeems me from death and crowns me with love and tender mercies. He fills my life with good things. My youth is renewed like the eagle’s!

Psalm 103:2 to 5 · NLT

Jeremiah 29:11 was first sent to people in a long wait. It is still a kind word for a new year of life. For more on looking ahead with God, see God’s plan and purpose and hope.

For cards

Birthday blessings.

If you searched for bible verses for birthdays blessing, these short lines are ready to write. They grow from the scriptures above. Copy one, then add a sentence in your own voice.

May the Lord bless you and protect you this year. May he smile on you and be gracious to you, and give you peace.

A blessing from Numbers 6:24 to 26

You are wonderfully complex, and his workmanship is marvelous. Today I thank God for the care with which he wrote your life.

A blessing from Psalm 139:14

May the year ahead hold his plans for good, and a future filled with hope.

A blessing from Jeremiah 29:11

May he grant your heart's desires and make all your plans succeed.

A blessing from Psalm 20:4

Whatever is good and perfect is a gift coming down to us from God. Today we thank him for the gift of you.

A blessing from James 1:17

May he reward you with a long life and give you his salvation.

A blessing from Psalm 91:16

A card does not need many sentences. One blessing, one memory, and a verse is enough. If you are celebrating a wedding year instead, see wedding Bible verses and verses for marriage.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Scripture never treats a birthday as a small thing. It numbers days, blesses years, and calls each person fearfully and wonderfully made. The kindest cards do two jobs at once: they thank God for the life, and they ask him to keep it. Whether you write to a child, a parent, or a friend, let the verse carry the weight and let your own sentence make it personal.

Keep a favorite blessing on a note in your phone for the next invitation. Or open the Jesus Forever Yours app and send someone the day’s verse with a line of your own.

A prayer

Lord, thank you for this life and for another year of it. Bless them and keep them. Make your face shine upon them, and give them peace. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What are the best Bible verses for a birthday card?

Numbers 6:24 to 26, Psalm 139:14, Jeremiah 29:11, and Psalm 20:4 are among the most loved. This page gathers twenty three passages, plus short birthday blessings you can copy into a card.

What Bible verse is a blessing for a birthday?

The priestly blessing in Numbers 6:24 to 26 is the classic scripture blessing: “May the Lord bless you and protect you.” Psalm 91:16 and 3 John 1:2 also read naturally as a prayer over someone’s year.

Which verse is good for a child’s birthday?

Psalm 139:13 to 14 thanks God for forming us with care. Jeremiah 1:5 and Ephesians 2:10 also remind a child that their life is known and made on purpose. Add one sentence about what you love in them.

Which Bible translation is used here?

Every verse on this page is quoted from the New Living Translation. The Jesus Forever Yours app also includes the NLT and KJV, with more translations in Pro.
