Scripture

Wisdom for studying.

Exams ask a lot of the mind. Scripture honors learning: wisdom is a gift, study is a craft, and a teachable ear is already a kind of success.

For the LORD giveth wisdom: out of his mouth cometh knowledge and understanding.

Proverbs 2:6 · KJV

Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

Proverbs 4:7 · KJV

The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge; and the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge.

Proverbs 18:15 · KJV

As for these four children, God gave them knowledge and skill in all learning and wisdom: and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams.

Daniel 1:17 · KJV

Teach me good judgment and knowledge: for I have believed thy commandments.

Psalm 119:66 · KJV

Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

2 Timothy 2:15 · KJV

If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

James 1:5 · KJV

A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels:

Proverbs 1:5 · KJV

This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

Joshua 1:8 · KJV

Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

Psalm 119:105 · KJV

Daniel and his friends studied in a demanding place, and God gave them skill. Faith and diligence belong together. For a wider look at wisdom, see Bible verses about wisdom and hard work.

Scripture

When test anxiety rises.

A racing heart before a paper is not a failure of faith. These verses give the fear somewhere to go: a God who is present, a spirit of a sound mind, a peace that keeps the heart.

Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.

Philippians 4:6-7 · KJV

For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

2 Timothy 1:7 · KJV

Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.

Isaiah 41:10 · KJV

Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.

Psalm 46:10 · KJV

Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

Joshua 1:9 · KJV

Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.

1 Peter 5:7 · KJV

What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.

Psalm 56:3 · KJV

Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

Isaiah 26:3 · KJV

Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.

Matthew 6:34 · KJV

In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

Psalm 94:19 · KJV

Be still, then begin. Cast the care, then open the booklet. If anxiety is loud beyond exam season, continue with Bible verses for anxiety, fear, and peace.

Scripture

Leaving the results.

After the work is done, waiting can be as hard as the test. These verses place the outcome with the Lord, without making effort pointless.

Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.

Proverbs 16:3 · KJV

Commit thy way unto the LORD; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.

Psalm 37:5 · KJV

And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.

Romans 8:28 · KJV

And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men; Knowing that of the Lord ye shall receive the reward of the inheritance: for ye serve the Lord Christ.

Colossians 3:23-24 · KJV

The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the LORD.

Proverbs 21:31 · KJV

And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

Galatians 6:9 · KJV

Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God.

1 Corinthians 10:31 · KJV

The horse is prepared, but safety is of the Lord. Work heartily as unto him. Then rest. A mark measures a moment. Your worth is held in a love that does not change with any score.

A closer look

A short reflection.

Scripture never treats learning as small. Proverbs calls wisdom the principal thing. The psalms ask to be taught. Paul tells Timothy to study as a workman who need not be ashamed. None of that is a promise that every exam will feel easy. It is a promise that God is in the work, in the fear, and in the result.

A helpful rhythm is simple. Begin a study session with one verse. Take honest breaks. On the morning of the test, sit still with Psalm 46:10, pray the prayer below, and start. When the results come, give thanks or grieve honestly, then keep walking. God is not waiting to love you more for a higher mark.

Write one verse on a card for your desk. Share one with a classmate. Or open the Jesus Forever Yours app for a daily letter, verse, and prayer — a quieter start than another hour of notes.

A prayer before an exam

God, thank you for a mind that can learn. Help me remember what I have studied. Quiet my fear, clear my thinking, and let me do this work with a steady heart. Whatever the result, I rest in your love. Amen.

Questions

A few common questions.

What Bible verse is good before an exam?

Psalm 46:10, Philippians 4:6-7, and 2 Timothy 1:7 are steady verses before a test. Pray the short prayer on this page, then begin. Faith is not a substitute for study; it is a way to carry the study.

How can I pray about test anxiety?

Name the fear, then read 1 Peter 5:7 or Isaiah 41:10. Ask God for a sound mind and a calm body. Breathe slowly. Remember that a score measures a moment, not your worth.

What if the results are disappointing?

Proverbs 16:3 and Romans 8:28 do not pretend every paper is easy. They place the work, and the outcome, in God’s hands. Work heartily. Then rest. Your value does not rise and fall with a mark.
