Instant Delivery

Hardcover Shipping

All hardcover orders are processed and fulfilled by our print partner. Please allow 4–6 weeks from the date of purchase for your order to arrive. You will receive an email confirmation when your order ships, including tracking information.

We currently ship to all US addresses. International shipping availability may vary please contact us before purchasing if you are outside the United States.

Please note: Shipping timelines are estimates and may be affected by carrier delays, holidays, or high order volume. We appreciate your patience.

Digital Delivery

Digital purchases (PDF format) are delivered instantly to the email address provided at checkout. If you do not receive your download within a few minutes, please:

  • Check your spam or junk mail folder
  • Ensure you entered your email address correctly
  • Contact us at support@jesusforeveryours.com

Returns & Refunds

Hardcover books: If your book arrives damaged or defective, please contact us within 14 days of delivery with a photo and we will send a replacement at no cost.

We do not accept returns of undamaged hardcover books once they have shipped, as each book is printed to order.

Digital products: All digital sales are final due to the nature of instant digital delivery. If you experience a technical issue with your download, please contact us and we will resolve it promptly.

Order Changes & Cancellations

Because orders are sent to our print partner promptly after purchase, we are unable to modify or cancel hardcover orders once they are placed. Please review your order carefully before completing your purchase.

Digital orders cannot be cancelled once the download link has been delivered.

Contact Us

Questions about your order? We're happy to help. Email us at support@jesusforeveryours.com or use our Contact page. We respond within 24 hours.
